# Changelog

All notable changes to Enhanced Inventory. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## 0.5.0 - unreleased

### Added

- Pages for big containers: a compartment of what you loot (a crate, a vehicle) taller than "Rows per page of a container" (`einv_gui_pageRows`, per player, 4 to 39, 10 by default, 0 or 40 for no pages) shows that many rows at a time. A bar under it turns the pages: a round arrow button at each end (its surface fades light under the mouse and out again, dimmed when there is no page that way; the hit area is the button and 2 pixels around it), and the bar says in one line where you are and what lies elsewhere - "Page 2 / 5 · +37 items on other pages", with "· 12 queued" when moves of the hands' queue wait on tiles of other pages. On the last page the bar itself says "Last page · 256 items on earlier pages", with the arrow back lit and its icon in the accent, so a box that opens on its last page is not taken for an empty one; a click on the words jumps to the first page (a last page that would hold only the spare rows after the last item is no page: the rows join the page before it). Nothing lies over the tiles. A page ends above a tile that would be cut, so every tile lies whole on one page. The pages replace the folded empty rows for such a box: there is no "+32 empty rows" line under it and the pages end two rows after the last one in use. A dragged item held over the outer third of the bar on either side turns the page after a short moment (and again while it stays), so it can be let go on a page that is not shown; let go on the box or on the middle of the bar it lies in the first free spot of the whole container, as on the fold line. Quick move, Take all, Sort and placing use the whole container - the pages are only the view - and only the shown page's tiles and cells have controls, which makes a crate with hundreds of items much lighter for the engine to draw. Turning a page draws only that box. The page is kept for the container for the mission. The wheel keeps scrolling the column (a column with several boxes needs it); your own gear is never paged; selecting (Ctrl+A, Shift+click) works on the shown page.
- The search finds an item on any page: the search field (the tiles that do not match fade, as before) now looks at every page of a paged box. Typing turns each such box to its first page with a match when the page it shows has none (it stays while it still shows one); the bar's arrows wear a small mark with the number of matches before and after the page, the bar's text counts the matches on other pages, and emptying the field gives back the pages the search moved (not one you turned yourself).
- Drop on a key you choose: a CBA keybind in Configure Addons (`einv_gui_drop`, "Drop (inventory window)", G by default, next to the quick move). It drops the selection, else the tile under the mouse, takes off and drops the item of a worn slot, empties a hotbar slot and takes the item out on ACE Arsenal's panel; the footer shows the key you set and leaves the hint out while it is unbound.
- The hands' queue: what takes the hands waits its turn, shown only in the caption line of the box it comes from. The quick move (F, double click), Take all, Put all in and dropping with the drop key or Drop in the menu each go one item after another, the hands' time per item; the caption line (the bar with Take all and Sort) gets a small chip: a ring that goes round, "Grabbing items 3 / 12" (or "Putting items away ..." for Put all in and the quick move into a box, "Dropping items ...", "Moving items ..." between own containers, "Changing gear ...") and a red cross that cancels what still waits of that box. The waiting tiles are tinted in the accent colour and their picture fades; they have no ring, number or circle (a click on one, or Cancel in its right click menu, still takes it out of the queue, and closing the window cancels what still waits). **A drag never waits**: what you let go of lands at once, within a container, onto a slot, between containers and onto the ground, and so does a group dragged together. When an item is sent it goes exactly as before, so multiplayer works as it did - nothing is reserved or changed before, and a move that fails (the item is gone, no room left) shows its hint while the queue goes on. Rearranging within a container, turning, splitting a stack and ACE Arsenal's panel stay instant. Server settings on the wizard's General page, under "Looting and searching": the three times "Time to drop an item" (`einv_gui_handDrop`, 0.3 s), "Time to move to another container" (`einv_gui_handMove`, 0.4 s) and "Time to put on or take off" (`einv_gui_handEquip`, 1 s), 0 for no wait; and four switches for what waits at all - "Quick move and Take all wait" (`einv_gui_waitMove`, on), "Dropping waits" (`einv_gui_waitDrop`, on), "Uniform, vest, backpack and helmet wait" (`einv_gui_waitWorn`, off: they are put on and taken off at once) and "Equipping other items waits" (`einv_gui_waitEquip`, off). The times do not depend on an item's weight.
- Searching, as Tarkov shows it: an item not searched yet is a diagonally striped tile in its real footprint (no "?", no ring and no magnifying glass on it). How far the search is shows only in the caption line of its box: a ring that goes round, "Searching 0:07" (the time that is left) and a red cross that stops the search of that box; a stopped box says "Stopped 0:07" and its button reads "Search" - it takes the search up again from the next item. The tiles are revealed one by one as before.
- A strip of categories at the side of a loot box (not on your own containers): All, weapons (with their parts), ammunition, gear and clothing, medical, food and drink, tools and electronics, other. A click shows ONLY that kind of item in the box - the rest is hidden, and the cells it lay on show as the empty cells they look like (the search field still dims what it does not find, on what the category leaves: a tile has to match both); a paged box shows only the pages that hold a match - its bar counts them ("Page 1 / 2" is the first of the two pages with a match), its arrows turn to the next page with a match and are off when none lies that way, and a category with no match in the box says "Nothing of this kind here" - and turns to the first page with a match, the bar saying how many matches lie on other pages; a click on the same icon, or on All, shows everything and gives the box back the page the choice moved it from - also while another box still has a category chosen. Hidden items are still in their cells (a drop from another box on such a cell lays the item in the first free spot of the box, as the box's picture and the bar's middle do - never a swap or merge with the hidden item) but the mouse does not find them. The strip is one rounded bar the colour of the caption chips; the chosen one is a lighter field with a white picture (no accent colour) that ends exactly where the bar ends, round at the bar's top and bottom corners; the mouse resting on an icon lights its field a little and shows its name. The strip takes its room from the box's picture (which gives way in half cells), not from the grid, and is left out where the column cannot spare it.
- Tile scale (`einv_gui_cellScale`, 0.8 to 1.3, per player): larger or smaller cells and pictures, from the next opening. Where both columns would no longer fit the screen the largest scale that fits is used and the RPT says so once; ACE Arsenal's panel caps it for itself so its ten columns still fit beside ACE's list.
- Rounds on magazines (`einv_gui_magazineText`, per player, beside the fill bar): off hides the "17/30" on magazine tiles and keeps the fill bar.
- Where other storage opens (`einv_gui_lootSide`, per player, "Enhanced Inventory - Client", General): by default your own storage column stays at the right edge and the column of what you loot (the ground, a crate, a vehicle, a body, what lies nearby) opens left of it, in the place between your equipment and your storage, so nothing you were looking at moves when a pile appears; "Right of my storage - my storage moves aside" brings back the old order, with the loot column at the right edge and your storage moving in. The camera on the soldier moves to the narrower middle in both without ending, so third person stays; kit windows, drags between the columns and quick move work as before. An open window follows a changed setting at its next build.
- Camera when opening (`einv_gui_cameraDefault`, per player): the soldier (third person, as before) or off (first person). The header's camera button still turns it for the open window; the choice is no longer remembered in the profile.
- The RPT names why the window closed, one line each time: `window closed: <reason>` (a movement key, Escape, the Gear key, the close button, the unit died or passed out, the vanilla inventory under it, another mod or script, ...).
- Weight: the CBA setting "Weight tiers" (`einv_weight_enabled`) switches the tiers off completely - no sprint block, no walking only, no extra fatigue, no tier messages; the weight card shows only the kilograms and ACE's own fatigue works alone.
- ACE interaction "Inventory" on other people (new optional addon `compat_ace_interaction`, loads with ACE's interaction menu): opens them as the action menu's "Inventory" does, under the same "Living persons" settings and reach.
- The rules selector `all`, everything else (`all > size=1x1 stack=10`, `all kind=uniform > grid=5x5`), which is asked after the preset and before the built-in answer, so it fills only the gaps - a rule, a table, a mod's config key or a preset rule with a condition still wins; a preset's own rules without a condition (its everything else) give way to it. The setup wizard's General page writes one for each kind of container.
- ACE interaction "Inventory" on vehicles (cars, tanks, static weapons, helicopters, planes, boats; `compat_ace_interaction`): opens the window on the vehicle's cargo as the action menu's "Inventory" does, shown only on foot within reach, for a vehicle with a cargo space or a grid of its own that is not locked to the player.
- Kits of Enhanced First Aid Kits and pouches of Enhanced Utility Pouches get cells of their own if you want: a new container kind `kit` (the kit types that mod registers, prototypes and the instances it makes of them), with a "Kits" chip in the setup wizard's Containers list, kits in its tree view and the same Paint / Blocks editor, Export and Import. Without a grid of its own a kit stays as it was - Enhanced First Aid Kits' capacity limits what goes in and its window's grid grows ("Now: capacity 35 (EFAK)"). Once a rule (`class=efak_IFAK+ > grid=4x3`, `kind=kit > grid=12`; the wizard writes the first, which reaches every kit of the type players carry) or the class's config key (`einv_grid = "4x3";`) gives it one, its cells are all its room: the capacity no longer counts in the inventory, in kit windows (the title says "8 / 12 cells") or in ACE Arsenal, where an item dragged from ACE's list onto a kit's tile in the panel is now packed into that kit (as many as the kit takes; the kit type's Arsenal setting in Enhanced First Aid Kits decides whether it may be filled there). Kits keep their sizes and stacks: `kind=item` and the presets' item groups still reach them, `kind=kit` reaches only kits. `einv_compat_efak_fnc_roomMode [kit]` says which counts, "capacity" or "cells".

- A picture of its own per item: the new rule value `picture=` (`class=My_Kit > picture=/A3/Weapons_F/Data/UI/gear_acc_rail_ca.paa`) and the item config key `einv_picture = "a3\ui_f\data\gui\cfg\hints\gear_ca.paa";` show another texture instead of the one the item's config names. A rules text writes the path with forward slashes, a space as `%20` and any other special character as `%XX` (a backslash typed into the rules box now becomes a slash instead of being dropped); a file the game does not have falls back to the config picture and is named once in the RPT. Everything that draws the item uses it (tiles, slots, cards, the inspect card, the hotbar, a kit window's title), `getInfo` has the new keys `picture` (the one shown), `pictureOverride` and `configPicture`, and Export, Import, Review and the tree view's `class=X+` rule know it.
- The setup wizard's Items step has a Picture row under Name: the current texture path (the rules' own, else config's greyed), Pick... and Reset. Pick... opens a picker with a search over the pictures of every item the wizard indexes (by name, class or path, wildcards too; the catalog is built in the background and kept), a page of 6 x 4 thumbnails with the item's name under each - a click picks, a second click or Use picture takes it -, and a field for any texture path with a live thumbnail that checks the file exists. The preview tile, the canvas and the zoom block show the chosen picture at once; Apply writes `picture=`.
- Container pictures follow `icon=` and `picture=`: the box of a crate or vehicle in the inventory (and the ACE cargo cards) draws its picture - the rules' own, else the editor's - larger, smaller or moved and cut to the box, as tiles do; a uniform, vest or backpack box and a kit window's title picture do the same. The Containers step has the same Picture row, zoom and shift (Zoom and place) beside a live preview of the box as the window draws it, under the preview of the cells; Apply writes `icon=` and `picture=` with the cells, Back to the preset takes them away too.
- Export at the Items and Containers editors asks what for: "Config entry (for a mod)" is the config lines with the class name, as before; "Layout only (to share)" is a short text that starts with `einv-layout:` and holds the values alone (grid, size, stack, weight, name, icon, picture), no class name.
- Import at the editors no longer takes a foreign class: a layout, or config lines or class rules whose class this game does not have (another mod's, not loaded), apply their values to the class selected in the editor (the selected tree, when the edit is for one) and the result line says so. For classes the game has, a question asks "Apply to the selected class" or "Apply to the classes named in the text" - the selected class is the default when the text names one class - and a class the game lacks never gets a rule.
- Picture zoom and shift per item: the new rule value `icon=` (`class=My_Kit > icon=1.4`, `icon=1.4@0.1:-0.05`) and the item config key `einv_icon = "1.4@0.1,-0.05";` draw an item's picture 0.25 to 4 times as large in its tile and move it up to a full tile across and down (a share of the tile; the text uses a colon between the offsets because a comma ends a statement). Tiles, the dragged item (also turned with R, the offsets turning with it), slots, the equipment cards, the inspect card and the hotbar bar all draw by it, and a picture that would reach out of its tile is cut at the tile's edge. The setup wizard's Items step has a Picture block next to the preview - a zoom stepper with 50/100/150/200 % chips, Across and Down steppers and Reset - and the preview draws the real tile with the inventory's own routine, so Apply writes what was seen; Export, Import, Review and the tree view's `class=X+` rule know it. `getInfo` has the new key `icon` (`[scale, across, down]`, default `[1, 0, 0]`).
- Setup wizard, Picture row: the whole texture path is text in the field now - click it, Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C copy it (read only; Ctrl+C or Return gives the wizard its keys back) - and Pick... opens the picker (a long path used to be cut at its beginning).
- Setup wizard, Zoom and place: the steppers step 1 % (Shift and click: 10 %) instead of 5, and a small text box beside Zoom, Across and Down takes an exact number (25 to 400 %, -100 to 100 %), read on Enter or when the cursor leaves the box, cut to the range, put back if it is no number; the stepper, the preview tile and the chips follow. The quick chips stay.
- Setup wizard, picture picker: chips "Item icons", "Uniforms & gear", "Vehicles & boxes" and "All" under the search (a picture belongs to the group of the classes that own it; a crate's or vehicle's editor picture is catalogued too), opening on the group of the class being edited. It opens on the picture the class has now - the one set, else config's - ringed and on its page; the chip turns to All when that picture is in another group.
- Use from containers: three server settings, "Use items from the uniform / vest / backpack" (`einv_storage_useUniform`, `useVest`, `useBackpack`, category "Use from containers"), each Everything (default, as in the game), Only whitelisted or Nothing. Magazines, grenades, medicine, food, kits and radios that lie in a limited container are no longer *usable*: they wear a small padlock on their tile and the footer says "Cannot be used from the backpack" (or "Whitelisted: can be used from the backpack"); the hotbar will not bind them, counts only what is usable, and loads, opens or takes only from where it may ("Cannot be used from the backpack - take it out first"); the context menu leaves out Open radio, Use as active radio and CBA rows that use the item up; and the keyboard's reload key loads no magazine from a blocked container (the fullest allowed one is loaded with the game's own reload action, and with none a line names where to take one out). Weapons, attachments and clothing are never limited, nor are moving, equipping and dropping. The game's own throw key, ACE's own medical and self-interaction menus, a gamepad reload and scripts still reach every container - a mod cannot change that; the setting's text says so.
- A new rule value `use=` and item config key `einv_use` whitelist an item for containers that are set to Only whitelisted: `kind=grenade > use=backpack+vest`, `class=ACE_elasticBandage > use=uniform+vest+backpack`, `use=none` takes a broader whitelist back (a comma would end a statement in the rules text, so it joins with `+`; config also takes `"backpack,vest"`). `getInfo` has the new key `use` (the containers in the order uniform, vest, backpack), and Export, Import, Review and the tree view's `class=X+` rule know it.
- The setup wizard's Items step has a "Use from" row under Picture: a Uniform, Vest and Backpack chip each, on for the containers the item is whitelisted for, with a line of what the settings limit now ("Limited now: Backpack: only whitelisted."); Apply writes `use=`, an emptied list `use=none`, and Back to the preset takes it away.
- Use from containers, fourth option **Strict (hidden)** per setting (`strict`): Only whitelisted, and the blocked items - magazines, grenades, medicine, food, kits, radios that are not whitelisted for that container - are kept out of the container's cargo altogether, in a hidden holder on the unit (a public variable, `einv_storage_hidden`, no object), so nothing but Enhanced Inventory can reach them: not ACE medical or rations, not the engine's reload or throw, not ACE Advanced Throwing, not a script's `items`. They still show as the container's tiles with a small crossed eye after the padlock ("Hidden: kept out of the backpack, so nothing else can use it"), move, drop, go onto the ground and into crates and slots, weigh (their whole mass goes through ACE's virtual load) and keep their places; whitelisted items stay in the container and work as usual. A new op `strict` keeps holder and cargo in line on the unit's owner (what a script, a loadout or ACE puts into a Strict container is hidden a moment later; switching the setting back gives everything back), every few seconds for every unit here with a holder. On death everything goes into the body's containers (looting works), on taking a uniform, vest or backpack off into that item, while an arsenal is open on your machine into the containers (and back after). A CBA loadout set on the unit replaces the holder with the loadout; a container swapped, dropped or replaced by another mod takes its hidden things with it where it still exists. Honest limits in the setting's text: loadouts saved outside an arsenal lack the hidden items, radios and kits in the holder are unknown to their mods, a Zeus editing a remote player does not see them, and it may misbehave on respawn or with Zeus - use only if you need it. `einv_storage_fnc_hiddenCounts` and `fnc_hiddenClasses` give what a container's holder keeps, for anyone counting cargo by hand.
- Use from containers, tighter: a reload from the scroll-wheel action menu, a gamepad button or a script no longer keeps a magazine out of a closed container. The player's magazines are noted by id once a second and after every move (`fnc_useTrack`); when the game's "Reloaded" event shows that the loaded magazine came from a container that is closed to it (`fnc_onReloaded`), the reload is taken back a frame later (`fnc_reloadUndo`): the magazine goes out of the weapon and back into its container, the magazine the weapon had goes back in, every step is checked by counting so none is lost or doubled, a line says "Cannot reload from the backpack.", and the fullest magazine that may be used is loaded instead. The throw key holds back too (`fnc_throwGuard`): with only closed grenades left the game's Throw key does nothing and says where to take one out, a selected throwable that is closed moves on to one that may be thrown, and ACE Advanced Throwing's "Prepare grenade" is blocked through ACE's `blockThrow` status effect while nothing may be thrown (`fnc_throwTick`). The settings' texts and the README now list exactly what is enforced and what is not (ACE medical and rations and other mods that read the unit's items, ACE Advanced Throwing's choice of throwable, a closed copy of a type that also lies in an open container, scripts).
- Items swap places when you drag one onto another (Tarkov's behaviour): let go of a tile over a taken tile and, when both fit the other's place, the dragged one lands where you hold it and the other goes to the cell the dragged one came from - in one container or between two (own gear, crates, the ground, bodies and living persons by their settings), in one transaction through the hub (`exchange` in `fnc_sendMoves`: take both, put both, nothing lost or doubled; within one container a `placeMany`). The footprint of both tiles shows in the accent colour with "Swap places with <name>" in the footer. Only a plain tile on a tile of another class: the whole of a stack, the footprint over exactly one other tile, that tile finding room at the cell you came from (as it lies, else turned); repacking magazines, loading and attaching, and putting into a kit keep what they do (a stack of the same class is never swapped), and a pile on the ground never loses its last item that way.
- Hotbar: drag a filled slot of the bar to rearrange or remove it - let go on another slot and the two swap, let go anywhere outside the bar and the binding is removed (the item stays where it is), let go on the bar between its slots and nothing happens. The ghost fades where it would be removed and the footer says what letting go does. G (or Delete) over a slot still clears it.
- Shift+click on a stack splits it: half goes onto the nearest free cell of the same container as a stack of its own. Dropping on a free cell no longer joins a stack of the same class elsewhere in the container, and part of a stack let go in its own container is split off onto that cell. The storage keeps them apart now (see below); `einv_storage_stackSplits` switches the window's side of it off again, then Shift+click selects a range and drops join as before. Quick move and Take all keep filling partial stacks.
- Separate stacks in the storage: two stacks of one class lie apart in one container (5 of 10 on one cell and 1 on another stay two tiles). The places' records of a stackable tile carry how many it holds as an eighth element; `fnc_getContents` cuts the cargo along them and reconciles with what the cargo really holds, so an item added or taken by the engine, a script or another mod is never lost or doubled (surplus tops up the last stack with room, then opens new ones; missing items come from the last stacks first). Records without a count - older saves, loadouts - read as before. Part of a stack taken out lowers its own tile's count; a put without a cell joins the stacks with room first, which is what Quick move, Take all, Sort and the plan do. New op `split` (part of a stack to a stack of its own, on a cell or the free spot nearest to the source) and the options `separate` and `merge into` on the ops `put` and `move`; kits keep working without them.
- Dropping on a stack of the same class with room merges into that very stack: the footprint lies on it in the accent colour with "Add to the stack of ...", as many join as fit (all, or what the wheel left) and the rest stays where it lay, with no free cell needed. Within one container it is a take and a put into that stack in one transaction.
- Right click menu, "Settings for <name>" (only for whoever may open the setup wizard, `einv_setup_fnc_canOpen`): on a tile, a worn slot, the box of an opened uniform, vest or backpack and the free cells of a crate or vehicle. It closes the window and opens the wizard at that item's page - Containers for what holds things (uniform, vest, backpack, crate, vehicle, kit), Items for the rest - with the class picked. The step is found by its id in `einv_setup_steps`, so the page order may change.
- Setting "Item names on tiles" (`einv_gui_tileNames`, per player, General, off by default): the short name in the top corner of every tile. Off, the tiles show the picture alone (count, rounds and marks stay) and the name still shows at the mouse. ACE Arsenal's panel follows it; the setup wizard's preview always shows the name.
- Setup wizard, General: the first page, with the settings that hold for everything. Under *Containers* each kind - uniforms, vests, backpacks, vehicles, crates - is Auto, the size its load gives, or Fixed, every container of the kind the same size across x down (all uniforms 5x5 is one row; the rule `all kind=uniform > grid=5x5`, so a class with a rule of its own or a mod's `einv_grid` still wins), each with a small reset button back to Auto. *How room is worked out* explains in plain words, with the numbers as they are now, how a load turns into cells for each kind of container, how big an item is and what stacks, and holds the load per cell (`cell=`, with its reset). Import, Export and Reset to default are in its top row.
- Setup wizard, server settings: every setting of the mod that is the same for everyone (searching, living persons, weight, the hotbar, repacking, locks, the vanilla arsenal, ...) is a control of the General page - a switch, a stepper, a drop-down, a text box, grouped by topic, the description in the tooltip - with a small reset button while it is off its default, and no row of CBA's menu any more. They are saved in one CBA text box, `einv_main_settings` ("Server settings - set in the setup wizard, do not edit"), as `einv1, gui_searchTime=0.6, weight_enabled=0` - only what is off its default - by the server, the mission or a `cba_settings.sqf`, forced or not, like the rules. `einv_main_fnc_applySettings` reads it on every machine into the variables the mod always read, under the same names, as the same kinds of value, with the same scripts and CBA's setting-changed event: nothing that reads them changed. Settings are registered with `einv_main_fnc_addSetting` (the same arguments as `CBA_fnc_addSetting` less the category and the scope); a mission whose `cba_settings.sqf` still has lines for the old names, or that saved them in Eden, keeps them while the text box is empty (the mission's own only - the server's own profile and file are not read). What a player sets for themselves stays in CBA's menu.
- Setup wizard, a small reset button beside every setting of the General page and beside the size, stack, weight, name and use of an item and the cells of a container, there only while the value is not the default (what the class would be without a rule of its own for it); a click takes it back at once. The header's "4 changes" opens the list of what Save would store, with a cross to take each change back.
- Stack whitelist: the default preset lets a short list of consumables stack - bandages, tourniquets, injectors and small infusions of ACE and ACM ten to a tile, sutures, cable ties and earplugs twenty, rations, splints, bigger infusions, chest seals and first aid kits five, the heaviest infusions and blood bags two. The rules are keyed by class (`@stack_10: class=ACE_fieldDressing|ACM_IV_16g+|...`), so they match nothing where the mod is not loaded; a rule of the text for a class wins over them. Chalk, anything that is a magazine to the game and what keeps a state of its own (canteens, fresh blood bags) never stack.
- Container patterns `pouches` (magazine pouches, a tall 1x2 then a square 2x2, and so on, up to 20) and `block` (one block 6 wide) for `grid=pouches:20`, `grid=block`; they are what a vest and a uniform get when nothing says otherwise.
- Quick move on a key: F over a tile does what a double click does (moves it over, opens a uniform, vest or backpack lying in a container, moves a whole selection); the double click still works. The key is a CBA keybind in Configure Addons (`einv_gui_quickMove`, "Quick move (inventory window)") and the footer shows it as "F Quick move".
- A container whose empty rows are folded away ("+34 empty rows") takes what is dragged onto it anywhere: onto its box, the fold line under the grid, or a cell that does not take the item (taken, or the item taller than the rows shown), the item is laid down in the first free spot of the whole container, folded rows included - no need to unfold it first. The footprint shows the shown compartment at half strength and the hint says "Goes to the first free spot - the folded rows too".
- Popup windows can be dragged by their title bar: the wizard's picture picker, Export and Import (with their choice cards and text windows), Import classes, "Same as another class" and the list of changes, the confirm cards (closing with unsaved changes, resetting, deleting a preset, ...), ACE Arsenal's "Gear does not fit" card and the blocked-arsenal screen. Press on the bar (not on the close cross or a button) and move; the window follows, anything dimmed behind it stays, and it cannot leave the screen - the bar always stays reachable. A faint highlight shows the bar under the mouse. The wizard remembers where each window was let go for the rest of the session (the confirm cards open in the middle every time). Dropdown lists are not moved. Nothing runs while no window is dragged. The helper is `einv_gui_fnc_dragHandle`.
- Swapping two tiles by dragging one onto the other also works in the ACE Arsenal panel between the container and the staging space (a swap, or a tile joining a stack of its own class there); ACE is told of both classes. Within the open container it already did.
- The outline under the mouse is the square frame of the Ctrl selection (two pixels in the accent colour, no fill): every item tile in the grids (containers, the ground, vehicles, kits) and every free cell of those grids gets it when the mouse is on it. The weapon, attachment, worn gear (headgear, uniform, vest, backpack), small gear and hotbar slots keep a round ring with the corners of their own panel instead, as before. A tile gets it around its whole footprint (a long, tall, turned item too, a shaped item on its own cells), a free cell around that one cell. One outline at a time, made for what is under the mouse and gone when it leaves; it also goes while an item is held, where the footprint shows instead.

### Fixed

- The script error "Error max: Type String, expected Number" in `fnc_animateStatus` while grabbing or searching (Mia, 0.5.7): `max` binds tighter than `find`, so the verb of the hands' queue was asked for its maximum before it was looked up. Parenthesised; no other file has the pattern.
- Pages and filters: turning a page of a box with a search or a category on lit every tile up for a moment (the page's new tiles were made at full strength and faded to the dimmed state over a tenth of a second). A tile made new takes its state at once now.
- The sound dropdown's sliders only gave 0 and 100 % (Mia, 0.5.7): the slider read the mouse from the arguments of the control's press and the display's move, which were not in the space of the track's place, so every place was off the track. The mouse is read with `getMousePosition`, as everything else does, and the volume goes in steps of 5 % along the track (the knob's middle runs from one end of the track to the other).
- Clicking a button fast no longer takes the frame rate down (Mia, 0.5.6: spamming the fold button of a backpack went to 15 fps, a way to crash the game): every click built the whole window. The state a click changes (folded, rows opened, the camera, the order of Sort, the chosen category, the page wanted, the typed search) is changed at once, and what is heavy - building the window, drawing the kit windows, the paged boxes, the fade of the tiles, a Sort, Take all / Put all in, the sound dropdown - runs through one gate (`fnc_coalesce`): the first click runs at once as before, the clicks that follow within a rest of 0.1 s (0.3 s for requests to the authority and plans of a whole container; 1.5 times as long as the last run took when that is longer) are one run more, with the state the last of them left, at most one run waiting per button and container. A run of clicks on a page arrow draws the page the last click asked for, a typed search follows 0.15 s after the last key, and Sort writes the profile once instead of once per click. Nothing is queued per click, and the table is emptied with the window.
- Pages, search and mouse: after a page was turned (an arrow, a dragged item held over the bar, the search jumping to the page with the hit) the tiles on it were not the grid's own - the page was drawn from a copy of the tile list - so the search did not fade the tiles that do not match, the mouse showed no outline on them (the name and hint still came) and a Ctrl selection or the queue's marks did not find them. The tiles of a page are the grid's own HashMaps now; the page's hover geometry is worked out again after every turn, and a tile that has lost its box is boxed again where its cells lie before the outline is drawn. The outline of a move waiting on another page of a box no longer lies over what is there.
- Scrolled down in a column, folding or unfolding a section (or any redraw of the window) made the list jump to its top for a moment: a group made anew starts at the top and learns its height a frame later. A column scrolled down and drawn again in the same place with the same title now keeps its panel, its title and its scroll group; the new sections are drawn into the group first and what stood in it goes in the same call (as the wizard's General page does), and the scroll is put back at once and a frame later.
- Pages of a big container showed through each other: faint tiles of another page lay under and over the tiles of the page shown (a tester's crate on page 3 of 3), and the orange "+180 items on other pages" chip covered the first row's tiles. The chip and the fade over the page's edge are gone (the count is in the bar under the box, "Page 3 / 3 | +180 items on other pages | 12 queued"), nothing lies over the tiles any more. The controls a tile made are now all deleted when its page is left or its box drawn anew - the grid keeps a list of every control its tiles made, so even one a tile no longer names (the faded copies the search field's fade had touched) is cleared with the page - and drawing a tile over controls it still holds deletes them first, and every redraw of a paged box deletes a control that belongs to no tile (the RPT says "A paged box held N controls that belong to no tile - deleted"). Only the shown page has tiles, cell squares and marks of the hands' queue; what waits on tiles of other pages is counted on the bar.
- A move that finished lit up or touched more than it had to: the colour of every kept tile was set again on each redraw (only a tile the mouse had lit is now), the flash that shows where an item landed stayed on the window invisible for good (hundreds after a long Take all; it is deleted when it has faded), the page bar was drawn anew on every redraw (it is left alone while what it says is the same) and the numbers of the hands' queue counted down on every waiting tile once a second (above, under Changed).
- Holding the quick move key (F) over a big container no longer drags the frame rate down (from about 280 to 25 fps in a tester's crate) and no longer sends more than one move per finished wait. A held key repeats, and every repeat asked a lot: it laid the three target containers out anew for the room (and the move once more), looked through the whole queue for the tile, searched the grids for it again for the mark and drew the queue's marks from the first entry to the last at every frame; on a backpack or a kit in a crate each repeat even opened or closed it and built the window. Now the first press of a hold does what a double click does, the repeats only queue; a tile that waits or is on its way costs one look; the room of a container is worked out once per change of the container (`fnc_roomFor`) and the room the waiting moves will take is counted, so a held key stops queuing where the container is full instead of queuing what would bounce; the queue is indexed by cell and the marks are drawn for the new entries only. The server (and other players) only ever get one move per finished circle - the key repeats never leave the window - and, with the hands' times at 0, a tile asked for again before the answer is no longer sent twice.
- Third person view lost as soon as an item was dropped: the camera on the soldier was ended and made anew whenever the free middle of the window changed - the pile that a drop makes on the ground opens the loot column and the middle moves - and ending a camera ("terminate") leaves the player in first person. A camera that already frames the soldier is now moved and re-aimed instead, and whenever it ends (window closed, camera switched off, a vehicle) the view the player had before it began (third person, first person, gunner) is switched to again.

## 0.4.0 - unreleased

### Added

- ACE Arsenal panel: a grid panel on ACE's own display for the selected uniform, vest or backpack tab. Items ACE adds land on the grid, tiles are re-sorted by drag, a tile dropped outside leaves the container, and an add with no free cell is taken back with ACE's message.
- Arsenal staging space: a grid under the container that holds items while the soldier rearranges; it is deleted with ACE's window and grows with its contents.
- Arsenal list drag and wheel: a row of ACE's list can be dragged straight onto a cell, the wheel sets the count while dragging, Ctrl+wheel over a tile adds or removes one. The panel's sections fold.
- Backpack as the fourth slot of the gear row (headgear, uniform, vest, backpack).
- Shaped grids: `grid=` takes a mask (`grid=xxxx/x--x+2x3`, `x` a cell, `-` a hole) and a whole container can be one canvas with a letter per compartment (`grid=abbbbbc/abbbbbc/-bbbbb-`), drawn where it was painted.
- Shaped items: `size=xx/x-` makes an L of three cells; the footprint follows the shape in both orientations, and R turns it. Place records gain an optional rotation.
- `like=CLASS`: a class takes another class's resolved size, stack and grid.
- Config keys for mod authors, written exactly like the rules: `einv_grid = "1x3+5x7+1x3";` (any `grid=` value, shapes and canvases too) on a custom uniform, vest, backpack, crate or vehicle class, and `einv_size = "2x1";` (also a mask like `"xx/x-"`), `einv_stack = 10;`, `einv_weight = 0.5;` (kilograms, as `weight=`), `einv_name = "Short name";` on any item. A subclass inherits them as config does. They sit below the server's rules text and above the presets; the older `einv_gridSize[]`, `einv_stackSize`, `einv_grids[]` and `einv_slots` still work, the new keys win. A value that cannot be read is named in the RPT once.
- `einv_items_fnc_configEntry [class]` writes the config lines of a class from what it is now (only the keys that differ from what the class gets without the rules text, from its own config and the preset), and `einv_items_fnc_parseConfigEntry [text]` reads such lines back, one class or several - for the wizard's export and import.
- Grids up to 10 cells wide.
- Presets Default and Realism. Default gives containers 20 % more room; Realism is the former Tarkov preset and stacks as Default does.
- Setup wizard: paint the compartments with colours, a Blocks mode for the block-by-block editor, "Same as another class" (follow with `like=` or copy the cells), a plain preset choice on Start with the cell size under Advanced, a welcome page and a "What you changed" list that removes a rule or jumps to its class.
- Setup wizard, Items: a Weight field (kilograms, as `weight=`) and a Name field next to size and stack, with the same Apply, Discard and Back to the preset; a field emptied takes its own value away.
- Setup wizard, Containers and Items: Export copies the config lines of the picked class (`class CfgVehicles { class B_Carryall_blk { einv_grid = "1x3+5x7+1x3"; }; };`, worked out from the draft, so a design can be exported before Save; for an edit of a whole tree one line per class), and Import applies pasted config lines - or rules, as Review's Import reads them - to the draft in one undo step.
- Setup wizard, class lists: the search takes wildcards (`B_Bergen*`, `*Carryall*`, `?` for one character), and a List | Tree switch shows a class with every class built on it as an indented tree with counts ("Up one level" starts it at the parent, which finds a family that shares a hidden `_Base` class). A class picked in the tree is edited with everything built on it: Apply writes one rule `class=X+ > ...` instead of one per class, a class with a rule of its own further down keeps it, and the lists say which rule covers a class ("your rule", "inherited from X", "preset").
- Window details: a right-click on another tile while a menu is open moves the menu, and counts and fill texts sit on a dark pill so white pictures do not hide them.

### Changed

- Cells are the only room: what fits into a container is decided by free grid cells alone. Weight is still shown and drives the weight bar and ACE fatigue, but no move is refused because of it.
- The own arsenal window is replaced by the panel inside ACE Arsenal (the vanilla Virtual Arsenal handling stays, with "ace" in place of "enhanced").
- The weight bar loses its yellow and red ticks: the fill colours by tier and the texts name the next limit and its effect on movement.
- Stackable tiles show n/stack in a flat box bottom right, nothing for a stack of one.
- Backpacks with load 0 take an own grid and are listed by the wizard.
- The engine's own commands (`addItem`, `addItemToUniform`, `addMagazine`, `addWeapon`, loadouts, ACE, mission scripts, the arsenals) no longer overfill a container: whatever a unit's uniform, vest or backpack holds beyond its grid is moved on to the next of its containers with free cells (uniform, then vest, then backpack) by the machine the unit is local on, a moment after its cargo changed - on the player's loadout change, the engine's Take / Put / InventoryClosed, a loadout set, a changed rule set and while a window shows the overflow, bodies and looted persons included. What fits nowhere stays overflow, as before; nothing is dropped or copied. Nothing moves while ACE Arsenal or the vanilla arsenal is open (the arsenal gives back what has no free cell), and once it closes the surplus moves on before the overflow is reported.
- The wizard has four steps: Start, Items, Containers, Review.
- The window is the only inventory. The unit's `InventoryOpened` handler always keeps the vanilla one away (on a respawn, a team switch and in Zeus remote control as well), and a vanilla inventory that another mod or script brings up anyway is closed at once - by its load event and by a check every quarter second, with the window open or not. Crates, the ground, vehicles, bodies and persons open here for every player.
- A window that cannot be created opens nothing (the RPT says why) instead of falling back to the vanilla inventory.
- Section captions: "Take all", "Put all in" and a new "Sort" button (the context menu's Sort row stays) stand beside the tab as high as it and in its text size, with the labels centred in their buttons both horizontally and vertically - the engine centres them now, so the button no longer reads lopsided.
