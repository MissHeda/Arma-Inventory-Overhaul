# Changelog

All notable changes to Enhanced Inventory. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## 0.4.7 - unreleased

### Added

- Tile scale (`einv_gui_cellScale`, 0.8 to 1.3, per player): larger or smaller cells and pictures, from the next opening. Where both columns would no longer fit the screen the largest scale that fits is used and the RPT says so once; ACE Arsenal's panel caps it for itself so its ten columns still fit beside ACE's list.
- Rounds on magazines (`einv_gui_magazineText`, per player, beside the fill bar): off hides the "17/30" on magazine tiles and keeps the fill bar.
- Camera when opening (`einv_gui_cameraDefault`, per player): the soldier (third person, as before) or off (first person). The header's camera button still turns it for the open window; the choice is no longer remembered in the profile.
- The RPT names why the window closed, one line each time: `window closed: <reason>` (a movement key, Escape, the Gear key, the close button, the unit died or passed out, the vanilla inventory under it, another mod or script, ...).
- Weight: the CBA setting "Weight tiers" (`einv_weight_enabled`) switches the tiers off completely - no sprint block, no walking only, no extra fatigue, no tier messages; the weight card shows only the kilograms and ACE's own fatigue works alone.
- ACE interaction "Inventory" on other people (new optional addon `compat_ace_interaction`, loads with ACE's interaction menu): opens them as the action menu's "Inventory" does, under the same "Living persons" settings and reach.
- Setup wizard: "Everything else" on the Start page - one size and stack for items no rule answers, and a grid for uniforms, vests and backpacks no rule answers, with Apply and Clear. It writes the new rules selector `all` (`all > size=1x1 stack=10`, `all kind=uniform|vest|backpack > grid=6x4`), which is asked after the preset and before the built-in answer, so it fills only the gaps; a preset's rules without conditions (Default's `size=1x1 stack=1`) give way to it. Review words it.
- ACE interaction "Inventory" on vehicles (cars, tanks, static weapons, helicopters, planes, boats; `compat_ace_interaction`): opens the window on the vehicle's cargo as the action menu's "Inventory" does, shown only on foot within reach, for a vehicle with a cargo space or a grid of its own that is not locked to the player.
- Kits of Enhanced First Aid Kits and pouches of Enhanced Utility Pouches get cells of their own if you want: a new container kind `kit` (the kit types that mod registers, prototypes and the instances it makes of them), with a "Kits" chip in the setup wizard's Containers list, kits in its tree view and the same Paint / Blocks editor, Export and Import. Without a grid of its own a kit stays as it was - Enhanced First Aid Kits' capacity limits what goes in and its window's grid grows ("Now: capacity 35 (EFAK)"). Once a rule (`class=efak_IFAK+ > grid=4x3`, `kind=kit > grid=12`; the wizard writes the first, which reaches every kit of the type players carry) or the class's config key (`einv_grid = "4x3";`) gives it one, its cells are all its room: the capacity no longer counts in the inventory, in kit windows (the title says "8 / 12 cells") or in ACE Arsenal, where an item dragged from ACE's list onto a kit's tile in the panel is now packed into that kit (as many as the kit takes; the kit type's Arsenal setting in Enhanced First Aid Kits decides whether it may be filled there). Kits keep their sizes and stacks: `kind=item` and the presets' item groups still reach them, `kind=kit` reaches only kits. `einv_compat_efak_fnc_roomMode [kit]` says which counts, "capacity" or "cells".

- A picture of its own per item: the new rule value `picture=` (`class=My_Kit > picture=/A3/Weapons_F/Data/UI/gear_acc_rail_ca.paa`) and the item config key `einv_picture = "a3\ui_f\data\gui\cfg\hints\gear_ca.paa";` show another texture instead of the one the item's config names. A rules text writes the path with forward slashes, a space as `%20` and any other special character as `%XX` (a backslash typed into the rules box now becomes a slash instead of being dropped); a file the game does not have falls back to the config picture and is named once in the RPT. Everything that draws the item uses it (tiles, slots, cards, the inspect card, the hotbar, a kit window's title), `getInfo` has the new keys `picture` (the one shown), `pictureOverride` and `configPicture`, and Export, Import, Review and the tree view's `class=X+` rule know it.
- The setup wizard's Items step has a Picture row under Name: the current texture path (the rules' own, else config's greyed), Pick... and Reset. Pick... opens a picker with a search over the pictures of every item the wizard indexes (by name, class or path, wildcards too; the catalog is built in the background and kept), a page of 6 x 4 thumbnails with the item's name under each - a click picks, a second click or Use picture takes it -, and a field for any texture path with a live thumbnail that checks the file exists. The preview tile, the canvas and the zoom block show the chosen picture at once; Apply writes `picture=`.
- Container pictures follow `icon=` and `picture=`: the box of a crate or vehicle in the inventory (and the ACE cargo cards) draws its picture - the rules' own, else the editor's - larger, smaller or moved and cut to the box, as tiles do; a uniform, vest or backpack box and a kit window's title picture do the same. The Containers step has the same Picture row, zoom and shift (Zoom and place) beside a live preview of the box as the window draws it, under the preview of the cells; Apply writes `icon=` and `picture=` with the cells, Back to the preset takes them away too.
- Export at the Items and Containers editors asks what for: "Config entry (for a mod)" is the config lines with the class name, as before; "Layout only (to share)" is a short text that starts with `einv-layout:` and holds the values alone (grid, size, stack, weight, name, icon, picture), no class name.
- Import at the editors no longer takes a foreign class: a layout, or config lines or class rules whose class this game does not have (another mod's, not loaded), apply their values to the class selected in the editor (the selected tree, when the edit is for one) and the result line says so. For classes the game has, a question asks "Apply to the selected class" or "Apply to the classes named in the text" - the selected class is the default when the text names one class - and a class the game lacks never gets a rule.
- Picture zoom and shift per item: the new rule value `icon=` (`class=My_Kit > icon=1.4`, `icon=1.4@0.1:-0.05`) and the item config key `einv_icon = "1.4@0.1,-0.05";` draw an item's picture 0.25 to 4 times as large in its tile and move it up to a full tile across and down (a share of the tile; the text uses a colon between the offsets because a comma ends a statement). Tiles, the dragged item (also turned with R, the offsets turning with it), slots, the equipment cards, the inspect card and the hotbar bar all draw by it, and a picture that would reach out of its tile is cut at the tile's edge. The setup wizard's Items step has a Picture block next to the preview - a zoom stepper with 50/100/150/200 % chips, Across and Down steppers and Reset - and the preview draws the real tile with the inventory's own routine, so Apply writes what was seen; Export, Import, Review and the tree view's `class=X+` rule know it. `getInfo` has the new key `icon` (`[scale, across, down]`, default `[1, 0, 0]`).
- Setup wizard, Picture row: the whole texture path is text in the field now - click it, Ctrl+A and Ctrl+C copy it (read only; Ctrl+C or Return gives the wizard its keys back) - and Pick... opens the picker (a long path used to be cut at its beginning).
- Setup wizard, Zoom and place: the steppers step 1 % (Shift and click: 10 %) instead of 5, and a small text box beside Zoom, Across and Down takes an exact number (25 to 400 %, -100 to 100 %), read on Enter or when the cursor leaves the box, cut to the range, put back if it is no number; the stepper, the preview tile and the chips follow. The quick chips stay.
- Setup wizard, picture picker: chips "Item icons", "Uniforms & gear", "Vehicles & boxes" and "All" under the search (a picture belongs to the group of the classes that own it; a crate's or vehicle's editor picture is catalogued too), opening on the group of the class being edited. It opens on the picture the class has now - the one set, else config's - ringed and on its page; the chip turns to All when that picture is in another group.
- Use from containers: three server settings, "Use items from the uniform / vest / backpack" (`einv_storage_useUniform`, `useVest`, `useBackpack`, category "Use from containers"), each Everything (default, as in the game), Only whitelisted or Nothing. Magazines, grenades, medicine, food, kits and radios that lie in a limited container are no longer *usable*: they wear a small padlock on their tile and the footer says "Cannot be used from the backpack" (or "Whitelisted: can be used from the backpack"); the hotbar will not bind them, counts only what is usable, and loads, opens or takes only from where it may ("Cannot be used from the backpack - take it out first"); the context menu leaves out Open radio, Use as active radio and CBA rows that use the item up; and the keyboard's reload key loads no magazine from a blocked container (the fullest allowed one is loaded with the game's own reload action, and with none a line names where to take one out). Weapons, attachments and clothing are never limited, nor are moving, equipping and dropping. The game's own throw key, ACE's own medical and self-interaction menus, a gamepad reload and scripts still reach every container - a mod cannot change that; the setting's text says so.
- A new rule value `use=` and item config key `einv_use` whitelist an item for containers that are set to Only whitelisted: `kind=grenade > use=backpack+vest`, `class=ACE_elasticBandage > use=uniform+vest+backpack`, `use=none` takes a broader whitelist back (a comma would end a statement in the rules text, so it joins with `+`; config also takes `"backpack,vest"`). `getInfo` has the new key `use` (the containers in the order uniform, vest, backpack), and Export, Import, Review and the tree view's `class=X+` rule know it.
- The setup wizard's Items step has a "Use from" row under Picture: a Uniform, Vest and Backpack chip each, on for the containers the item is whitelisted for, with a line of what the settings limit now ("Limited now: Backpack: only whitelisted."); Apply writes `use=`, an emptied list `use=none`, and Back to the preset takes it away.
- Use from containers, fourth option **Strict (hidden)** per setting (`strict`): Only whitelisted, and the blocked items - magazines, grenades, medicine, food, kits, radios that are not whitelisted for that container - are kept out of the container's cargo altogether, in a hidden holder on the unit (a public variable, `einv_storage_hidden`, no object), so nothing but Enhanced Inventory can reach them: not ACE medical or rations, not the engine's reload or throw, not ACE Advanced Throwing, not a script's `items`. They still show as the container's tiles with a small crossed eye after the padlock ("Hidden: kept out of the backpack, so nothing else can use it"), move, drop, go onto the ground and into crates and slots, weigh (their whole mass goes through ACE's virtual load) and keep their places; whitelisted items stay in the container and work as usual. A new op `strict` keeps holder and cargo in line on the unit's owner (what a script, a loadout or ACE puts into a Strict container is hidden a moment later; switching the setting back gives everything back), every few seconds for every unit here with a holder. On death everything goes into the body's containers (looting works), on taking a uniform, vest or backpack off into that item, while an arsenal is open on your machine into the containers (and back after). A CBA loadout set on the unit replaces the holder with the loadout; a container swapped, dropped or replaced by another mod takes its hidden things with it where it still exists. Honest limits in the setting's text: loadouts saved outside an arsenal lack the hidden items, radios and kits in the holder are unknown to their mods, a Zeus editing a remote player does not see them, and it may misbehave on respawn or with Zeus - use only if you need it. `einv_storage_fnc_hiddenCounts` and `fnc_hiddenClasses` give what a container's holder keeps, for anyone counting cargo by hand.
- Use from containers, tighter: a reload from the scroll-wheel action menu, a gamepad button or a script no longer keeps a magazine out of a closed container. The player's magazines are noted by id once a second and after every move (`fnc_useTrack`); when the game's "Reloaded" event shows that the loaded magazine came from a container that is closed to it (`fnc_onReloaded`), the reload is taken back a frame later (`fnc_reloadUndo`): the magazine goes out of the weapon and back into its container, the magazine the weapon had goes back in, every step is checked by counting so none is lost or doubled, a line says "Cannot reload from the backpack.", and the fullest magazine that may be used is loaded instead. The throw key holds back too (`fnc_throwGuard`): with only closed grenades left the game's Throw key does nothing and says where to take one out, a selected throwable that is closed moves on to one that may be thrown, and ACE Advanced Throwing's "Prepare grenade" is blocked through ACE's `blockThrow` status effect while nothing may be thrown (`fnc_throwTick`). The settings' texts and the README now list exactly what is enforced and what is not (ACE medical and rations and other mods that read the unit's items, ACE Advanced Throwing's choice of throwable, a closed copy of a type that also lies in an open container, scripts).
- Items swap places when you drag one onto another (Tarkov's behaviour): let go of a tile over a taken tile and, when both fit the other's place, the dragged one lands where you hold it and the other goes to the cell the dragged one came from - in one container or between two (own gear, crates, the ground, bodies and living persons by their settings), in one transaction through the hub (`exchange` in `fnc_sendMoves`: take both, put both, nothing lost or doubled; within one container a `placeMany`). The footprint of both tiles shows in the accent colour with "Swap places with <name>" in the footer. Only a plain tile on a tile of another class: the whole of a stack, the footprint over exactly one other tile, that tile finding room at the cell you came from (as it lies, else turned); repacking magazines, loading and attaching, and putting into a kit keep what they do (a stack of the same class is never swapped), and a pile on the ground never loses its last item that way.

### Changed

- Colours: the leftover blue-tinted darks and greys are neutral like the inventory window now. The setup wizard's popovers and menus (Import, Export, the right-click menu) use the theme's inspect background (near-black instead of blue-black), the Containers canvas's filled cell and its hover are neutral greys, and every colour a window falls back to when the theme lacks a key (text, text 2, muted, button text, on-accent ink, tooltip, inspect and panel backgrounds, in the inventory, the wizard, the ACE cargo cards and the weight card) is the theme's own neutral value. The arsenal's blocked screen and close confirmation dim and card are black and the theme's panel instead of blue-black. The weapon tile tint, the compartment paint colours and the info blue stay.
- Setup wizard: only the server, a hosted game's host, single player and logged-in admins see it at all (`einv_setup_fnc_canOpen`, no setting): the inventory's cog is not drawn for anyone else, CBA's Addon Options button is hidden, the keybind does nothing and ESC > Options > Inventory setup closes the window again without a word. Eden always opens it. The setting "Who may open the setup" (`einv_setup_access`) is gone; whoever may not change the setup no longer sees it read-only. An admin who logs in or out is noticed at the next draw of the header or the menu.
- Closing the wizard with unsaved changes asks "You have unsaved changes." in a card centred on the wizard, its words centred and its answers right-aligned with the same gap: "Save and close" (saves as Save at Review does, then closes; not offered where saving is not allowed), "Close without saving" (in the loss colour) and "Keep editing", which Escape is as well. The old "Close" / "Cancel" pair that read as closing the popup is gone.
- The context menu holds only what belongs to what is under the mouse. A tile: Inspect, Equip / Attach to / Load into where they apply, Drop, Select all in its container, and the item's own rows (Open kit, Unpack everything, Open radio, Use as active radio, Repack all, the hotbar's, other mods' CBA rows, Open arsenal on an ACE arsenal box). A worn slot: Inspect and Drop (to the ground), a weapon's Take off for each part, the radio rows. A selection of several tiles: Deselect. Free cells: Select all. Moving over is the double click and the drag, dropping is Delete, taking a worn item off is Ctrl+click, sorting is the Sort button.
- The Sort button on a section's caption line and on a kit window's title row cycles through the orders and says which it sorts by: "Sort: Name", "Sort: Weight" (heaviest first), "Sort: Size" (largest first, as Sort always did) and "Sort: Type" (weapons, ammo, gear, medical, the rest). A click steps to the next order and sorts by it, every Sort button on the window changes its words at once, and the choice is kept per player in the profile. The container is sorted where it is local, so everyone looking into it sees the same places.

- Lifting an item no longer drops the frame rate: the green highlight of the slots that take it is made once per window (ahead of time, while nothing is held) and only shown and hidden by a drag instead of some fifteen new controls per slot, the soldier's loadout is read once instead of once per weapon part, what a weapon takes is looked up in config once, a click-selected tile that is dragged keeps its frame instead of drawing it again, a dragged group makes its extra footprints only when it can keep its arrangement, and while an item is held the slot, weight forecast, mass and free-cell answers are worked out once per target instead of every frame.
- "Log window timings (debug)" also writes, when the window opens with it on, `timing: frames idle 0.06 ms avg ... / hover held ... / hover same ... / hover new ... / drag ...` (the script's cost of a frame while idle, with the mouse over tiles and dragging, every 1500 frames, at a drag's start and when the window closes), the controls on the display by class with each build (`timing: build 12 ms (1850 controls on the display, 1210 of them shown: EINV_PanelRect 900, ...)`), and the drag line now starts `timing: drag`. What a frame costs beyond that script time is the engine drawing the display.
- Holding an item no longer costs frames while it is held. Where it would land is worked out only when something changes - the mouse comes onto another cell, slot or panel, the count or the turn changes, a column scrolls. A mouse that stands still costs nothing; one that moves inside a cell moves the picture and nothing else. Where the slots and grids lie on the screen is read once per drag (again after a column scrolled), which cells of a grid are taken once per grid. "Log window timings (debug)" writes one line per drag instead of `timing: lift N ms`: `drag: start 2.3 ms, frames 412, update avg 0.04 ms max 1.31 ms (slowest: ...)`.
- Painted containers (a canvas, `grid=aab/acc`, the setup wizard's Paint) show where one compartment ends and the next begins. Where two compartments touch, the drawing opens up along that whole column or row line by the room flowed compartments have between them (about a fifth of a cell), so the compartments keep their painted places relative to each other, line up as painted, and every cell keeps its one size and the normal gap; a compartment beside an empty place, or one whose neighbour only touches it at a corner, keeps its cells where they were. A compartment that reaches across such a line (a wide one under two narrow ones) has that room between two of its own cells. Every compartment, painted or flowed, now lies on a faint rounded panel of its own behind its cells (a shaped one's holes left open), so painted and flowed containers look alike; a canvas with such lines comes out a little wider and taller than its cells. Tiles, footprints, selection frames, the selection band and the mouse use the same places, and the room between two compartments is no cell. The setup wizard's "This is how it looks" preview draws it the same way, with smaller cells where a wide canvas would not fit.

### Removed

- The context menu rows "Move over", "Move N items over", "Drop N items", "Sort <container>" and "Take off" (a worn slot keeps "Drop"; "Take off" put the item into the player's own gear first, which Ctrl+click on the slot still does).

### Fixed

- Picture picker: the picture in use is found by its whole path (lowercase, slashes unified, leading slash dropped - `einv_setup_fnc_pictureKey`) and only the card of that file is ringed and turned to. A crate or vehicle with no picture of its own is drawn by its editor picture, so that is the one in use now, not its config picture (the white silhouette). A file shared by several classes is one card, and a class whose list picture and editor picture are the same file counts once in "+N".
- ACE Arsenal, "Gear does not fit" card: the wheel no longer moves the card, both answers act when clicked, and the click sound plays only on an answer. The glass over the window was a focused button: the engine draws the focused control over every other and gives it the mouse first, so it lay over the two answers - they got no clicks and no hover, and every press anywhere played the button sound. The glass is now a plain dimming rectangle, the focus goes to the focus sink (a control of no size) while the card is up, and the card, dimming and answers are made straight on the display instead of in a controls group (which the wheel scrolled by the pixel its content overran it). The display itself takes the answers' clicks (press and release on the same answer) and the wheel while the card is up, as it already did their hover; Escape is still Keep editing.
- A crate or vehicle picture in a box (inventory, ACE cargo cards, the setup wizard's preview) no longer smears when it is zoomed and moved: the box's three pieces moved the picture by a share of their own width and height instead of the box's, so each showed a different slice.
- ACE Arsenal, "Gear does not fit" card: the labels of Keep editing and Remove and close sat below the middle of their buttons and the buttons did not light up under the mouse. The label of every centred button (the card's, ACE cargo's, the section captions') now sits in a box as high as its text with its middle on the button's, so it stands in the middle whichever way the engine places the line, and the card's two answers are lit up from the mouse's place every frame as well as by the buttons' own events, which did not reach them on ACE's display. The setup wizard's buttons (its "You have unsaved changes." card, the picture picker's Use picture, Close and pager among them) are laid out the same way - the label centred by the engine in the middle of the button, an icon beside it at the left padding - instead of by its measured width.
- ACE Arsenal: things that no longer fit after a smaller vest, uniform or backpack was put on stay as red tiles while the window is open, but no longer in the gear after it: Close and Escape ask first ("N items do not fit your gear and will be removed: ...", Keep editing / Remove and close), and Remove and close takes exactly those out, as the panel's Remove does. A window closed by something else removes them right after, with a line in the RPT and a message on the screen (they used to be moved on to another container or stay as red overflow outside the arsenal).
- Setup wizard: percent signs were missing in words - "picture at 220 , moved 20  across and 0  down" in What you changed and Review, "Zoom 100" on the steppers, "Collecting pictures... 40" - because the engine's `format` drops a lone `%`; they read "picture at 220 %, moved 20 % across and 0 % down", "Zoom 100 %" and so on.
- Setup wizard: a line of What you changed (and of Review's list) for a crate or vehicle opens the Containers step with that class picked, whatever it sets - a rule with only a name, picture or zoom opened the Items step, which lists no crates or vehicles. A uniform, vest, backpack or kit opens Containers for a rule with `grid=` or `like=` or with nothing of an item's own (size, stack, weight, name), Items otherwise.
- The camera button in the header is white like the other header icons (it was the accent colour while the camera was on) and shows the camera off by dimming the icon. Where a server or mission forces "Camera when opening" (a CBA force line) the button is no longer drawn: there is nothing for the player to switch.
- Hovering costs fewer frames: the places of all grids, slots and info areas are kept instead of walked again for every mouse move (the groups they lie in are read only to notice a scroll), only the grids under the mouse are asked for a cell, and a mouse moving inside the tile or slot it was on ends the lookup at once. The idle frame skips calls that had nothing to do (the context menu's raise while none is open, the search animation with nothing searched, the slot highlights once all are made, the window hook when none attached).
- The window closing at once with SFX Project: Remastered (workshop 2129532219) loaded, and with any other mod whose own InventoryOpened handler comes after this window's: the engine then opens the vanilla inventory as well, the guard closed it again a frame later with a bare close that could land on the window made in between (the window hung under that display and went with it, "window closed: from outside ... exit code 2" in the RPT). Now the window is not made while a vanilla inventory the guard closed is still held, that second close goes through the guard which sees where the window stands, the window's handler is put behind the other mods' handlers again (a few seconds after the mission display exists, on every unit change, and whenever a vanilla inventory came up although the handler had answered - the RPT says so), and a vanilla inventory nobody asked the window for opens the window in its place. The other mods' handlers are left as they are, so their sounds go on.
- The name at the mouse is no longer covered by the accent ring of a slot: the ring round the slot under the mouse (the Backpack slot's, over "Carryall Backpack (Coyote)") was made after the name and drawn over it; the name, and the comparison card, are now made again over anything made after them.
- Turned items: the picture of a turned tile, and of a dragged item held turned (also from ACE Arsenal's list), turns a quarter clockwise round the tile's middle and fits the turned box, instead of showing squashed and out of its tile.
- The context menu no longer hides behind the tiles: it is a layer of its own that takes the focus and is made again on top when anything is drawn after it; a menu on a tile whose crate was redrawn by another player's change closes. A section's button no longer keeps the focus after its click.
- The window closing at once with some mods loaded: when another mod lets the engine open the vanilla inventory as well, the window no longer opens on top of it (made a frame later as its child, it was closed along with it); the guard closes only the vanilla inventory (IDD 602), never the window or another mod's display, and opens the window again should it have been closed with it.
- ACE Arsenal: dragging from ACE's list into the grid works on the first press: the row is taken where the press was, the drag starts after 5 pixels in any direction (up and down too), and a press is no longer forgotten in the frame it came. A press on ACE's "-"/"+" never drags; an item ACE would not add says "ACE does not allow this item here" instead of doing nothing.
- ACE Arsenal: the panel's title, its name and the hint line are sized in ACE's own grid, as ACE's list beside them, instead of the inventory window's units, which stood a size off ACE's texts on some screens.
- ACE Arsenal: the list no longer goes grey after a click on a row. ACE looks at its list again at every pick and greys every row by the container's weight (a backpack with a load of 0, like ACE's Telescopic Ladder given cells by the rules, refuses everything), and only the "+" of the row picked was lit again; now the whole list is lit again by free cells a frame after each pick, as it is after an add. What the grid has room for stays lit; adding already ignored the weight.
- The small stutter on every move: an answered move draws again only the sections it touched (and the weight card and hotbar counts when it was the soldier's own gear) instead of building the whole window, the camera on the soldier is no longer restarted by every build or fold, and ACE's weight arriving a moment later redraws only the weight card. "Log window timings (debug)" (`einv_gui_debugTiming`, per player, off) writes build and redraw times to the RPT.
- A magazine dragged into the weapon in the hands plays the weapon's reload animation again (with or without the looting animation; in the looting pose the soldier straightens up for it), not only its sound.
- The weapon no longer comes out of the inventory lowered: closing the window gives the soldier his weapon back as he held it when it opened.
- Another person's night vision slot is captioned "Night vision" (was "Eyewear", which read as the goggles' slot).
- Painted compartments (a container drawn as a canvas, `grid=aab/acc`) show every cell the same size with the same gap to the next, in the inventory window, in kit windows and in the setup wizard's "This is how it looks" preview. Cells next to another compartment were drawn in by half the compartment gap, so they came out narrower than the cells inside a block, one-cell compartments came out tiny, and the gaps and frames overlapped or were cut at the seams. A canvas is now one uniform grid of cells, a compartment is just the cells it covers (where two touch, the drawing opens up between them - see Changed), and tiles, footprints, selection frames and the mouse all use that same cell geometry. The wizard's preview, and the Paint and Blocks editors, also take their cell and padding in whole pixels, so no cell is a pixel wider than its neighbour and no gap a pixel wider than another.
- Vehicles: seated in one, a movement key no longer closes the window (W, S, A and D are the vehicle's there), and moves in a driving vehicle are always in reach; something opened from a seat outside the vehicle closes once out of reach. An opening that names nothing to loot - a vanilla inventory that comes up without the unit's handler being asked, a script's `action ["Gear", objNull]` - opens on the vehicle the player sits in or looks at (in reach, not locked) instead of only the soldier's own gear. The RPT names what each opening asked for (`asked: ...`), what the window opened on with its grid (`open: <class> kind <family> rows x cols ...`) and why one was refused (`refused: ...`).
- Vehicle inventories show the vehicle again (0.4.9: the Gear key or the action menu's "Inventory" on a vehicle could open the window with no loot column, only the soldier's own gear). Since 0.4.9 a vanilla inventory that comes up without the unit's handler having been asked opens the window in its place on a guess; when that display came up before the engine's event, the guess was queued ahead of the engine's own opening of the vehicle and won, and the vehicle's opening found a window open and was dropped without a word. What the engine names now always wins: the guess is made a frame later and only when the handler was not asked in the frame the vanilla inventory came up or since and no other opening waits for it, it gives way while it waits for the display to go, and a window already open on a guess closes for the engine's opening and opens again on the vehicle (`replaced: ...` in the RPT). A queued opening that finds a window open says so (`refused: a window is open already - <class> ...`).
- Kit windows (IFAKs, pouches): the title row is laid out like a section's caption line. Take all and Sort are the caption's buttons - as high as a caption line, in its text size, their labels centred, as wide as on a section, Sort saying "Sort: Name" and changing with the order - and stand together on the right before the fill text and the cross, one gap between each; the picture, the name, the buttons, the fill text and the cross (now as high as the buttons) all sit on the row's middle line. Where "Sort: Weight" does not fit, Sort gets compact before anything goes - the sort icon and "Sort", then the icon alone, the order in its tooltip ("Sort: Weight - click for Name") -, then it is left out, then Take all, and only then is the name cut. Take all and Sort answer on a kit opened just now (the title's light no longer lies over them), a press on them no longer raises or moves the window and so loses no click, and Take all has the section's tooltip.
- ACE Arsenal: items in the staging space can be rearranged, turned and moved on again. A move that only changed the staging space (a tile moved or turned inside it) was never seen by the window - the staging crate is no section of the soldier's gear, which the window's check reads - so its tiles stayed where they were drawn and the next lift answered "Someone else was quicker"; only an add from ACE's list drew it anew. - ACE Arsenal: the picture the rules give an item (`picture=`, `einv_picture`) shows on ACE's own lists as well - the left list, the right list of the weapon tabs and the container tabs' list - instead of the config picture; the panel's tiles had it already.
- The editor's picture of a crate or vehicle (box in the loot column, ACE cargo cards, kit and Containers previews) no longer shows a lighter rectangle where the picture sits: ACE's previews lie on a lighter grey than BI's, and the box's backdrop could not be told it, so the edges of the image now fade into the backdrop's colour over a few pixels.
- A box picture zoomed by `icon=` over 1 can no longer be scrolled with the mouse wheel - the engine scrolled its clip group after the handler had put it back, so each turn left it one step down; the group is put back every frame for a few frames after a turn - and keeps the box's round corners: the picture is cut in three pieces that leave the corner squares to the backdrop, instead of one square group over them.

## 0.4.0 - unreleased

### Added

- ACE Arsenal panel: a grid panel on ACE's own display for the selected uniform, vest or backpack tab. Items ACE adds land on the grid, tiles are re-sorted by drag, a tile dropped outside leaves the container, and an add with no free cell is taken back with ACE's message.
- Arsenal staging space: a grid under the container that holds items while the soldier rearranges; it is deleted with ACE's window and grows with its contents.
- Arsenal list drag and wheel: a row of ACE's list can be dragged straight onto a cell, the wheel sets the count while dragging, Ctrl+wheel over a tile adds or removes one. The panel's sections fold.
- Backpack as the fourth slot of the gear row (headgear, uniform, vest, backpack).
- Shaped grids: `grid=` takes a mask (`grid=xxxx/x--x+2x3`, `x` a cell, `-` a hole) and a whole container can be one canvas with a letter per compartment (`grid=abbbbbc/abbbbbc/-bbbbb-`), drawn where it was painted.
- Shaped items: `size=xx/x-` makes an L of three cells; the footprint follows the shape in both orientations, and R turns it. Place records gain an optional rotation.
- `like=CLASS`: a class takes another class's resolved size, stack and grid.
- Config keys for mod authors, written exactly like the rules: `einv_grid = "1x3+5x7+1x3";` (any `grid=` value, shapes and canvases too) on a custom uniform, vest, backpack, crate or vehicle class, and `einv_size = "2x1";` (also a mask like `"xx/x-"`), `einv_stack = 10;`, `einv_weight = 0.5;` (kilograms, as `weight=`), `einv_name = "Short name";` on any item. A subclass inherits them as config does. They sit below the server's rules text and above the presets; the older `einv_gridSize[]`, `einv_stackSize`, `einv_grids[]` and `einv_slots` still work, the new keys win. A value that cannot be read is named in the RPT once.
- `einv_items_fnc_configEntry [class]` writes the config lines of a class from what it is now (only the keys that differ from what the class gets without the rules text, from its own config and the preset), and `einv_items_fnc_parseConfigEntry [text]` reads such lines back, one class or several - for the wizard's export and import.
- Grids up to 10 cells wide.
- Presets Default and Realism. Default gives containers 20 % more room; Realism is the former Tarkov preset and stacks as Default does.
- Setup wizard: paint the compartments with colours, a Blocks mode for the block-by-block editor, "Same as another class" (follow with `like=` or copy the cells), a plain preset choice on Start with the cell size under Advanced, a welcome page and a "What you changed" list that removes a rule or jumps to its class.
- Setup wizard, Items: a Weight field (kilograms, as `weight=`) and a Name field next to size and stack, with the same Apply, Discard and Back to the preset; a field emptied takes its own value away.
- Setup wizard, Containers and Items: Export copies the config lines of the picked class (`class CfgVehicles { class B_Carryall_blk { einv_grid = "1x3+5x7+1x3"; }; };`, worked out from the draft, so a design can be exported before Save; for an edit of a whole tree one line per class), and Import applies pasted config lines - or rules, as Review's Import reads them - to the draft in one undo step.
- Setup wizard, class lists: the search takes wildcards (`B_Bergen*`, `*Carryall*`, `?` for one character), and a List | Tree switch shows a class with every class built on it as an indented tree with counts ("Up one level" starts it at the parent, which finds a family that shares a hidden `_Base` class). A class picked in the tree is edited with everything built on it: Apply writes one rule `class=X+ > ...` instead of one per class, a class with a rule of its own further down keeps it, and the lists say which rule covers a class ("your rule", "inherited from X", "preset").
- Window details: a right-click on another tile while a menu is open moves the menu, and counts and fill texts sit on a dark pill so white pictures do not hide them.

### Changed

- Cells are the only room: what fits into a container is decided by free grid cells alone. Weight is still shown and drives the weight bar and ACE fatigue, but no move is refused because of it.
- The own arsenal window is replaced by the panel inside ACE Arsenal (the vanilla Virtual Arsenal handling stays, with "ace" in place of "enhanced").
- The weight bar loses its yellow and red ticks: the fill colours by tier and the texts name the next limit and its effect on movement.
- Stackable tiles show n/stack in a flat box bottom right, nothing for a stack of one.
- Backpacks with load 0 take an own grid and are listed by the wizard.
- The engine's own commands (`addItem`, `addItemToUniform`, `addMagazine`, `addWeapon`, loadouts, ACE, mission scripts, the arsenals) no longer overfill a container: whatever a unit's uniform, vest or backpack holds beyond its grid is moved on to the next of its containers with free cells (uniform, then vest, then backpack) by the machine the unit is local on, a moment after its cargo changed - on the player's loadout change, the engine's Take / Put / InventoryClosed, a loadout set, a changed rule set and while a window shows the overflow, bodies and looted persons included. What fits nowhere stays overflow, as before; nothing is dropped or copied. Nothing moves while ACE Arsenal or the vanilla arsenal is open (the arsenal gives back what has no free cell), and once it closes the surplus moves on before the overflow is reported.
- The wizard has four steps: Start, Items, Containers, Review.
- The window is the only inventory. The unit's `InventoryOpened` handler always keeps the vanilla one away (on a respawn, a team switch and in Zeus remote control as well), and a vanilla inventory that another mod or script brings up anyway is closed at once - by its load event and by a check every quarter second, with the window open or not. Crates, the ground, vehicles, bodies and persons open here for every player.
- A window that cannot be created opens nothing (the RPT says why) instead of falling back to the vanilla inventory.
- Section captions: "Take all", "Put all in" and a new "Sort" button (the context menu's Sort row stays) stand beside the tab as high as it and in its text size, with the labels centred in their buttons both horizontally and vertically - the engine centres them now, so the button no longer reads lopsided. - The ACE Arsenal panel is drawn like one of the inventory's columns: the inventory's glass and corner radius instead of ACE's black block, the container's picture and name in a title row of the columns' title size (the font no longer ACE's RobotoCondensed, 0.66 UNIT, centred on the picture) with a hairline under it, one padding (0.4 UNIT) round and between all bands so the picture, tabs, grids and loads sit on common edges, the staging subtitle in the caption's size, and the hint line in the inventory footer's size under a hairline.
